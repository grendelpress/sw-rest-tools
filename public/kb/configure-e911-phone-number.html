Introduction

SignalWire allows you to create and assign E911 addresses directly from your Space. The assigned address is provided to emergency services when someone calls 911 from that phone number.

The process has two parts: you must first create the emergency address, then assign it to each applicable phone number. Creating an address alone does not enable E911 on any number.



Create an E911 Address

1

Log in to your SignalWire Space

Sign in to your SignalWire dashboard with your credentials.

2

Select Addresses from the left navigation menu

From the main dashboard, open the Addresses section.

3

Click + New

Create a new emergency address entry.

4

Enter a label and the complete physical address

Provide a label and the full physical address where the phone number will be used.

5

Submit the address

Submit the address for validation. It must be validated before it can be assigned for E911 service.

📍
Use a complete and accurate address

Include any applicable apartment, suite, floor, or unit number. The address must be validated before it can be assigned for E911 service.



Assign the Address to a Phone Number

Creating an address does not automatically enable E911 for your phone numbers. You must assign the address to each number separately.

1

Select Phone Numbers from the left navigation menu

From the main dashboard, go to the Phone Numbers section.

2

Open the phone number you want to configure

Click on the specific phone number to open its settings.

3

Go to the number's E911 settings

Navigate to the E911 section on the phone number configuration page.

4

Locate the appropriate emergency address

Find the emergency address you created earlier from the list of available addresses.

5

Click Assign & provision

This submits the address to the carrier for provisioning.



Provisioning & Important Warnings

The address will be submitted to the carrier for provisioning. Provisioning may not complete immediately. Do not consider E911 active until the number's E911 status shows that provisioning has completed successfully.

⚠️
Emergency calls may be billed at $100 before provisioning

Until an emergency address is assigned and provisioned, 911 calls from the number may be routed through a national emergency call center and billed at $100 per call.

📍
Update the address if the number moves

If the phone number moves to a different physical location, update or replace its assigned E911 address promptly.



Configure E911 Through the REST API

You can also create and manage E911 addresses programmatically through the SignalWire REST API:

  • Assign an E911 address to a phone number

Provisioning Status Flow

When assigning an address through the API, provisioning begins with a pending status. The status changes to active after the carrier confirms the registration. If the carrier cannot complete the registration, the status changes to failed.

→
Active

Carrier confirmed

↳ Failed

if the carrier cannot complete the registration
